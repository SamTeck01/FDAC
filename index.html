About FDAC

More than a group chat

FDAC is a community created for people who want to grow, learn, take action, and achieve meaningful goals together.

This is not just a group where we share messages and move on. The goal is to create a space where members can discover opportunities, learn useful skills, share knowledge, support one another, and take real steps toward their goals.

Our Principle

Growth becomes easier when people support one another.

Who It's For

You don't have to have everything figured out before joining. What matters is the willingness to learn, improve, take action, and help others do the same.

Our Way

Focus → Learn → Act → Grow

Four steps that guide everything we do as a community.

01

Focus

Stay locked in on your goals and cut out unnecessary distractions.

02

Learn

Pick up useful skills, materials, and knowledge for today's world.

03

Act

Take on challenges that turn talk into real action and progress.

04

Grow

Celebrate wins, big or small, and keep building, consistently.

Our Goals

What we're here to do

Help members stay focused on their goals and avoid unnecessary distractions.

Share opportunities such as scholarships, training programs, competitions, internships, and jobs.

Share skills and resources that help members improve and become more useful in today's world.

Encourage learning and self-development through useful information, materials, and discussions.

Create challenges and activities that push members to take action, not just talk about their goals.

Build a supportive community where members share ideas, ask questions, and help one another.

Encourage discipline, consistency, good time management, and a strong work ethic.

Help members discover new areas they can explore and develop.

Celebrate progress and achievements, whether big or small.

Connect people with similar goals and interests so they can learn, work, and grow together.
